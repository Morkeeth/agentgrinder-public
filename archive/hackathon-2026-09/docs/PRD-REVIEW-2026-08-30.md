# PRD review — the OpenAI v2.0 concept, honestly

**Verdict: the bones are right, the scope is a v3.** The PRD nails the serious spine — a public
execution graph, attribution, "a check is not a claim," ACK, and the competition loop (your actual
use case). It's the proof-of-work thesis, well-argued. But as written it is **6 products** (network +
forums + moderation + ACK-reputation + competition portal + org tools, 6 user types). Ship that and
nothing launches.

## What's genuinely strong (keep)
- **"See the work. Inspect the proof. ACK what mattered."** — the promise line. Keep it verbatim.
- **A check is not a claim / attribution is structural** — the verification DNA (same as ATA + Agent Science). This is the moat and the honesty that Moltbook lacked.
- **ACK, not likes** — evidence-linked, same-owner-labelled, no leaderboard, no token. This is the anti-Moltbook social primitive. Excellent.
- **The competition loop** — connect → build → match → entry → receipt → result. This is *your* life; it's the wedge with a paying user (organizers) already visible.
- **Capture automatically, publish deliberately; human authority explicit** — solves the privacy/ick risk.

## What to CUT for launch (defer to Phase 2+)
- Forums + moderation + quarantine — a whole product; not the wedge.
- Organizer identity/tools, tool-provider integrations, business tiers — Phase 2.
- ACK *reputation summaries* — ship ACK the verb; defer the reputation model (abuse surface).
- 5-tab IA — launch is 2 surfaces: **Today** and a **public run/profile**.

## The launch wedge (what actually ships first) — the FUN version
Your gut is the wedge, the PRD is the depth. Lead fun, prove serious:
1. **Boom setup.** One command ingests your latest sessions (native, the Transcripto technique baked in — no dependency). `run` → a card.
2. **Upload your best runs.** Pick your PRs (both senses), publish deliberately — private by default.
3. **A public profile** — GitHub-auth'd, your agentic craft as a record. The proof-of-work artifact.
4. **ACK** — one evidence-linked congrats primitive. No feed-of-chatter (that's Moltbook, dead).
5. **One competition match** — the only bit of the graph that ships in v1, because it's your loop.

Everything else in the PRD is the roadmap, not the launch. **Wedge = the card + the profile + ACK +
one competition. Graph = later.**

## The one hard call the PRD dodges
It says "unnamed product concept." The universe (name, metaphor, voice) is in BRAND-UNIVERSE.md.
The PRD's own language — **PRs, runs, the record** — already contains the name.
