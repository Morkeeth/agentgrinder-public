# Agent Grinder · free and social

Build with agents. Show what you made. Find your people.

The first person is a daily Cursor builder who wants a recognisable public record of their work. They come back when another builder responds, follows them, or ships something worth trying.

## First release

1. Capture a Cursor session with one obvious action.
2. Preview a compact card: identity, project, trace, time and output, with unknowns left unknown.
3. Choose what to share. A useful output link and a short builder-written caption carry more meaning than token totals.
4. Open a public profile, follow a builder, discuss a run and give an ACK.
5. Return through responses in Inbox and the next run in My runs.

The existing cards/social code is the starting point. Output-link/caption polish and the Cursor entry action are build targets, not completed claims. Preserve the white card and blue trace design. Keep the first screen about people and their work.

## Free and clout-seeking

Make posting, profiles, following and recognition free. Let builders earn attention through real output and helpful discussion. Do not require the Strands coach, paid inference, a score dashboard or a competition workflow to participate. No fake activity, auto-ACK loops or fabricated shipped-work claims.

## First shared acceptance

Oscar and Eric each capture a safe real Cursor session. Each previews and deliberately posts a card to the independent service. One follows and replies to the other; the other sees the reply and returns. Record where help was needed. Two test accounts are not this result.

## Repository boundary

This repo owns public-product development. The original owns the hackathon. No automatic cross-repo sync or shared production database. Port an explicit fix when needed. The original deployment remains where the competition expects it.
